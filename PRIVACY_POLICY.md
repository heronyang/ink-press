# Privacy Policy for InkPress

Last updated: June 25, 2026

**InkPress** ("the Application") is a utility tool designed to convert web article URLs into clean, image-free EPUB documents.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
* **Personal Data**: The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). No registration, login, or user profiles are required.
* **Webpage Fetching**: When you paste a URL or share a link to the Application, the Application fetches the HTML content of that webpage directly from the destination server. This fetching occurs entirely on-device or via direct HTTP calls from your device. No third-party servers manage, log, or store the links you parse.
* **Storage**: Converted EPUB files are stored locally on your device in your public `Downloads/InkPress` directory. The Application does not upload your documents to any cloud servers.

### 2. Third-Party Services
The Application is built using the following open-source libraries:
* **Jsoup**: Used for on-device HTML cleaning and parsing.
No advertising networks, analytics trackers, or profiling SDKs are bundled with the Application.

### 3. Permissions Requested
* **INTERNET**: Required to download the content of the shared webpage URLs.
* **Storage Access (Legacy)**: On older Android versions, storage permission is used exclusively to write the output `.epub` files to your public Downloads directory. On Android 10 and above, the app uses the `MediaStore` API, which does not require storage access permissions.

### 4. Children's Privacy
The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
